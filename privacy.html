Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026 v2.0

ExecutionCurve is a personal persistence and project-logging platform. This policy explains what data we collect across all features: including the progress log, Navi AI coach, Roadmap, and Community — why we collect it, how it is stored, and your rights over it.

By using ExecutionCurve you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

1. Who we are

ExecutionCurve is an independent project. You can contact us with any questions about your data at executioncurve@gmail.com.

2. What data we collect

We collect only what is needed to make the app work for you:

  • Account data: your email address and username, collected when you create an account. Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text.
  • Journal entries: the text you write in each log entry, the feeling you select, and the date of the entry.
  • Project data: your project names, colors, and which project is currently active.
  • Goals: the goal you set for each project, used to personalize your AI feedback and roadmap.
  • AI nudges: the personalized next-step suggestions generated by Navi for each entry, stored alongside the entry.
  • Navi conversations: the full message history of your chat sessions with Navi, stored in your account so conversations persist across sessions.
  • Roadmap data: the AI-generated project roadmap associated with your goal and entries.
  • Community data: if you choose to submit a feedback request to the Community, your project brief and any context you share become visible to other logged-in users. Feedback you give to other users is stored and visible to the recipient.
  • Usage preferences: settings such as your last active project, stored so the app remembers your state between sessions.
  • Legal acceptance: a record of when you accepted each version of the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

We do not collect your name, phone number, physical address, or payment information. We do not run advertising and we do not sell your data.

3. How we use your data

Your data is used for one purpose: to make ExecutionCurve work for you.

  • Your email is used to secure your account and to send password reset emails.
  • Your journal entries, goal, and entry history are sent to Google's Gemini API to generate your personalized feedback (nudges), Navi responses, and roadmap. This data is transmitted securely over HTTPS.
  • Your entries are stored so you can see your timeline across sessions and devices.
  • Your Navi chat history is stored so conversations persist when you return to the app.
  • Community feedback requests and responses are stored so both parties can reference them.
  • Your legal acceptance records are stored to comply with our terms versioning policy.

We do not use your data to train AI models. We do not share your private journal entries, goals, or Navi conversations with any party other than the AI processing described above.

4. Third-party services

ExecutionCurve uses the following third-party services to operate:

  • Supabase — stores your account, journal entries, goals, projects, Navi conversations, roadmap, Community data, and preferences in a secure, US-hosted database with row-level security. Privacy policy: supabase.com/privacy.
  • Google Gemini API — generates AI responses for entry feedback (nudges), Navi conversations, and the personalized roadmap. Your entry text, goal, and recent history are sent to Google's servers for this processing. Privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Vercel — hosts the ExecutionCurve web application and serverless API functions. Privacy policy: vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy.
  • Google Fonts — serves the typefaces used in the app interface. Google may log font requests. Privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

We do not use advertising networks, analytics trackers, or social media pixels.

5. Data storage and security

Your data is stored in Supabase with row-level security enabled, meaning only you (or users you explicitly share with in Community) can access your records. All data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections. Passwords are hashed using industry-standard algorithms and never stored in plain text.

We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data but cannot guarantee absolute security. If you believe your account has been compromised, contact us immediately at executioncurve@gmail.com.

6. Community and shared data

Community participation is always optional. When you submit a feedback request, the brief you write becomes visible to other logged-in ExecutionCurve users until you remove it or receive sufficient responses. Feedback you receive is private to you. Feedback you give is visible to the recipient.

Do not include sensitive personal information in Community posts. Community posts that violate our Terms of Service may be removed.

7. How long we keep your data

Your data is kept for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, all associated entries, goals, projects, Navi conversations, roadmap data, and account information are permanently deleted. We do not retain backups of deleted user data beyond 30 days.

Legal acceptance records (which version of the Terms and Privacy Policy you accepted and when) are kept for as long as required by applicable law, even after account deletion.

8. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access all data we hold about you by emailing executioncurve@gmail.com.
  • Delete individual entries at any time using the entry controls in the app.
  • Delete all entries using the Start Fresh option in the app.
  • Request full account deletion by email — we will delete your account and all associated data within 7 days.
  • Correct inaccurate account information by contacting us.

9. Age requirements

ExecutionCurve is intended for users aged 13 and older. If you are under 13, please do not create an account. If we become aware that a user under 13 has created an account, we will delete it promptly. If you are between 13 and 18, we recommend reviewing this policy with a parent or guardian.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. When we do, we will update the version number and date at the top of this page. If the changes are material, we will ask you to review and accept the updated policy the next time you open the app before you can continue using it.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: executioncurve@gmail.com
